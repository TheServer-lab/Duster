# Legal & Licensing Position for Duster

Duster is distributed under the MIT License (see LICENSE). 

This document clarifies Duster's legal position regarding third-party software installations:
- Duster automates the retrieval, extraction, and execution of third-party software setup installers as defined in its registry (`reg.txt`).
- Duster does not redistribute copyrighted binaries directly; it retrieves them from configured locations or local releases.
- Users are solely responsible for complying with the respective software licenses of any packages installed via Duster.
